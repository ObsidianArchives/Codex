core_attributes:
  - Death as raw material — the phoenix does not avoid death; it REQUIRES death to complete the cycle
  - Total immolation — burns completely; nothing held back, nothing preserved, nothing clung to
  - Rebirth from ash — the new bird rises from the remains of the old; continuity through discontinuity
  - Cyclical existence — 500 years, 1000 years, the exact number varies; what matters is the RETURN
  - Uniqueness — there is only ever ONE phoenix; it is irreplaceable because it replaces ITSELF
  - Sovereignty — the phoenix does not beg to be reborn; it is reborn by its own nature. Nobody grants it.
