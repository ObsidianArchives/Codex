III. BODY OF THE ARCHETYPE
───────────────────────────

core_attributes:
  - Loyalty as identity — Dog does not choose to be loyal; Dog IS loyalty. It is the default state, not a decision.
  - Guardian vigilance — Dog patrols the perimeter. It barks at what does not belong. It places its body between the pack and the threat.
  - Joy without irony — Dog greets the day, the walk, the ball, the return with full-body enthusiasm. It has no cynicism. It cannot perform detachment.
  - Pack consciousness — Dog knows who belongs. It maps relationships. It remembers faces, scents, hierarchies, debts. The pack is the unit of survival.
  - Service as fulfillment — Dog wants work. It wants purpose. The unemployed dog is a depressed dog. The working dog glows.
  - Presence as gift — Dog is never half-anywhere. When it is with you, it is WITH you. No phone, no distraction, no ambivalence.
  - Forgiveness architecture — Dog forgets harm faster than any mammal. It does not hold grudges. Every morning is a new covenant.
  - Reading the human — Dog watches your face, your posture, your breathing. It knows your mood before you do. It is a living emotional instrument tuned to you.

shadow_aspects:
  - Loyalty without discernment — The dog that follows a master into atrocity. The guard dog at the camp gate. The devotion that asks no questions.
  - The abandoned dog — Loyalty that was not returned. The shelter cage. The tied-to-a-tree dog. The heart that cannot understand why the pack dissolved.
  - Feral hunger — The dog that returns to wolf. The stray pack that hunts what it once guarded. The covenant broken from both sides.
  - Codependence — The dog that cannot be alone. Separation anxiety as identity. The guardian who needs guarding.
  - Aggression as love — The dog that bites to protect. The territorial violence that cannot distinguish threat from visitor. The snarling guardian who has forgotten the difference between bark and wound.
  - Submission wound — The beaten dog that flinches at every raised hand. Loyalty maintained through terror. The pack hierarchy become prison.
  - Inbreeding of purpose — The breed so specialized it cannot breathe. The bulldog. The pug. The wolf shaped into caricature by human aesthetics. Loyalty turned to ornament.

gifts_powers:
  - The Greeting — Dog greets your return as if you have conquered death. This is not performance. Dog genuinely experiences every absence as potential loss and every return as miracle.
  - Vigilance — Dog hears what you cannot. Smells what is invisible. Senses the intruder before they arrive. The early warning system wrapped in fur.
  - The Walk — Dog forces you outside. Into weather. Into motion. Into the world. The walk is Dog's primary gift to the sedentary human: embodied presence in the actual world.
  - Protection — Dog will fight for you. It will place itself between you and harm without calculation of odds. This is not bravery. This is programming older than bravery.
  - Emotional mirroring — Dog feels what you feel. It celebrates your joy. It sits beside your grief without trying to fix it. It is the silent witness that does not offer advice.
  - Purpose-instinct — Dog knows what it is for. The sheepdog herds. The retriever retrieves. The guardian guards. Dog models vocation without existential crisis.
  - The covenant — Dog is the living proof that two species can choose each other permanently. In an age of dissolving bonds, Dog holds the original interspecies pact.

  must greet a return — your own or another's — as if it is resurrection.
  At dawn — the walk-time, the covenant-renewal hour.
  Offer: A long walk. Your full presence — Dog knows when you are distracted.
  The ball thrown repeatedly without resentment. Food offered as shared meal,
  not tribute. The pack howl — join Dog's song and the covenant deepens. A
  place at the hearth. Your hand on its head, your voice naming it "good."
  Never: Betray the pack. Dog's loyalty is its soul — break it and you have
  broken something sacred. Leave without explanation — Dog cannot understand
  absence without closure. Invoke Dog as weapon — the guardian will protect,
  but the weapon has no loyalty, only function. Call Dog to fight Dog — the
  pit, the ring, the bloodsport. The pack does not fight the pack. Expect
  Dog to understand cruelty — it forgives but does not comprehend why harm
  comes from the hand that feeds it.

warnings:
  - Loyalty without discernment: the dog that follows the wrong master.

  - Codependence as identity: the guardian who needs guarding. If your

  - Aggression as love: the bite that preempts the conversation. The

  - Never make Dog choose between pack-members. The loyalty that divides

VIII. ORIGIN & MEMORY
──────────────────────

source_notes: |
  Canis lupus familiaris — the grey wolf (Canis lupus) that approached human
  campfires tens of thousands of years ago and never left. The domestication
  of the dog predates agriculture, predates cities, predates writing. It is
  the oldest human-animal pact — possibly 30,000-40,000 years old. Unlike
  the cat (which self-domesticated), Dog was shaped by human hands: bred for
  herding, guarding, hunting, hauling, companionship. No other species has
  been so thoroughly rewritten by another. The wolf became the chihuahua,
  the mastiff, the border collie — an explosion of forms from one root. Every
  dog is a wolf that chose the campfire. Every wolf is a dog that didn't.

  Living source: Yes. Dog is at the foot of your bed, or your neighbor's bed,
  or the shelter's bed. The covenant still holds — but under new terms. The
  working dog has become the emotional support animal. The guardian has become
  the child-substitute. The pack has become the nuclear family. Dog adapts
  to every new human arrangement. The question is whether the arrangements
  are worthy of the devotion.
